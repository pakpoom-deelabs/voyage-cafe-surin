The Concept
"Every cup is a passport to somewhere beautiful…"

Tucked into the quiet neighbourhood of Ban Khok Krapho on the edge of Surin city, VOYAGE cafe' was born from a simple conviction: that great coffee should feel like an escape. Whether you're a local seeking a morning ritual or a traveller passing through Thailand's northeastern heartland, this is your perfectly brewed pause.

The space blends raw textures with considered light — exposed concrete softened by warm Edison glow, curated playlists drifting low, and a menu priced for everyone from students to explorers. Come once and you'll make it a habit.
